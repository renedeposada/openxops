What We Do

Open xOps connects people and organizations with practical software tools and experienced practitioners who deliver live, in-person training.

We help users find solutions, organizations find trainers, and tool creators and trainers reach the people who can benefit from their work.

What Open xOps Makes More Accessible

Practical Tools

Focused software tools intended to address specific real-world problems.

Experienced Trainers

Practitioners with real-world experience who deliver live, in-person training and communicate clearly.

Affordable Access

Tools and training designed to be more accessible to people and organizations.

Creator Opportunity

Tool creators and trainers can reach users and organizations and benefit from the value they provide.

Why Open xOps?

Practical solutions and experienced expertise can be difficult to access when people need them.

Open xOps exists to make those solutions and that expertise more accessible by connecting providers directly with users and organizations across many markets.
